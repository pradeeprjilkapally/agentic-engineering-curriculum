# Lessons

Notes from real mentoring sessions and real builds. The curriculum is the theory; this is what actually happened when people ran it.

This folder is the part that makes the repo *yours* over time. The curriculum could be anyone's. The lessons — the specific moment something clicked for someone, the failure mode you keep seeing, the exercise that didn't land — are the accumulated edge.

## What to log

- **A session** — who, what module, what clicked, what didn't, what you'd change.
- **A pattern** — something you've now seen enough times across people to name it.
- **A failure mode** — a specific way people get stuck, and what unsticks them.
- **A breakthrough** — the exact thing that flipped someone from vibe coding to agentic engineering.

## Format

One file per lesson. Use [`_template.md`](_template.md). Name files by date + slug: `2026-05-14-eval-first-clicks.md`.

## Why bother

Two reasons:

1. **It improves the curriculum.** Every "this exercise didn't land" is a curriculum edit waiting to happen. The lessons are the feedback loop.
2. **It's the moat.** Anyone can publish a curriculum. The person who has logged 50 real sessions knows exactly where people get stuck and exactly what unsticks them. That knowledge lives here.

## Privacy

Anonymize. Lessons are about patterns and what worked — not about naming who struggled with what. "An engineer three years into their career" not a name.
